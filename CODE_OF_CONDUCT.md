# Code of Conduct

RepoAura aims to provide a respectful and constructive space for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and changes rather than attacking people.
- Explain disagreements in good faith and remain open to evidence.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal or confidential information.
- Keep project discussions focused on improving RepoAura and its documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through GitHub. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

This repository does not identify individual maintainers or promise a response timeframe. If you cannot determine a private contact route, do not post sensitive details publicly.
